1. Overview

This page describes all forms of monetization offered on the Warbound DayZ server, including VIP Priority access and the in-game Points Shop (Shop Coins). It is published for transparency to players and to Bohemia Interactive as part of our server monetization approval request.

None of the items described below provide gameplay advantages such as increased damage, exclusive weapons, unlimited resources, or any other pay-to-win mechanic. All purchases are cosmetic, convenience-based (queue priority), or provide access to items available to all players through normal gameplay.

2. VIP Priority

VIP Priority gives paying players priority access to join the server when it is full. It does not grant any in-game gameplay advantage.

  • Duration: 1 month per purchase
  • Benefit: priority queue placement when the server is at capacity
  • Does not affect gameplay balance, loot, damage, or progression in any way
  • Payment: official PayPal Checkout (Goods & Services)

Server Launch Giveaway

As a one-time launch promotion, new players purchasing their first VIP Priority package receive an additional month free (2 months total for the price of 1). This is a limited-time offer for first-time buyers only and does not affect the standard VIP Priority terms above.

3. Points Shop (Shop Coins)

Shop Coins are a virtual currency that can be purchased and spent in the in-game Points Shop to unlock cosmetic items and rewards.

  • Exchange rate: €1 = 1 Shop Coin
  • Coins are added to the player's shop balance after payment is confirmed
  • Coins can be spent in the in-game Points Shop, accessible via our Discord
  • All items and crate contents are listed with their full contents in advance in the shop interface — no hidden or randomized rewards with undisclosed odds
  • Payment: official PayPal Checkout (Goods & Services)

4. Purchase Requirements

To receive any purchased item, VIP status, or redeem code, players must provide their Steam64 ID at the time of checkout. Purchases cannot be fulfilled without a valid Steam64 ID.

5. Payment Processing

All payments are processed exclusively through official PayPal Checkout links using PayPal's Goods & Services option. This ensures buyer protection and secure, verifiable transactions for every purchase. We do not accept Friends & Family transfers, cryptocurrency, or any other unverifiable payment method for shop or VIP purchases.

6. Refund Policy

Refund requests can be submitted via our Discord support channel within 14 days of purchase, provided the purchased item, coins, or VIP time has not yet been used or redeemed. In the event the server permanently shuts down, players with unused VIP time or unspent Shop Coins may request a pro-rated refund for the unused portion.

7. No Monetization Prior to Approval

In accordance with Bohemia Interactive's community monetization rules, no monetization of DayZ — including VIP Priority sales or Shop Coin sales — will occur until this application has been formally approved.

8. Support & Contact

For questions, disputes, or refund requests, players can contact server staff through our Discord server: discord.gg/warboundofficial
